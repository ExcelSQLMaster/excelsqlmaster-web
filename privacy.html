Overview

ExcelSQLMaster is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data is and is not collected in connection with the software and this website.

What We Do Not Collect

ExcelSQLMaster runs entirely on your machine. The software does not collect, transmit, or store any user data. There is no telemetry, no analytics, no usage tracking, and no cloud processing. Your data never leaves your machine. We do not operate servers that store customer information. We do not maintain a user database.

License Verification

When you open ExcelSQLMaster, the software contacts Paddle to verify that your license key is active. No personal data is sent during this process. Only your license key is transmitted to Paddle for validation.

Payment and Customer Data

All purchases, billing, and customer data are handled entirely by Paddle.com Market Limited, who acts as the Merchant of Record. Paddle collects your name, email address, and payment information to process your order and deliver your license. We do not have access to your payment details. Paddle's privacy policy governs how they handle your data and can be reviewed at paddle.com/legal/privacy.

Software Delivery

After purchase, Paddle sends you an email with a download link and your license key. We do not handle the delivery process.

Website

This website does not use cookies, tracking pixels, or any form of analytics. We do not collect any information about your visit to this website.

Third-Party Services

The only third-party service involved is Paddle for payment processing and license management. We do not integrate with any advertising networks, analytics platforms, or data brokers.

Data Processing Location

Since we do not collect or store personal data, there is no data transfer to any jurisdiction. Your data is processed locally on your machine. Paddle processes payment data in accordance with their own privacy policy and applicable data protection laws including the UK GDPR and EU GDPR.

Your Rights

Since we do not store your personal information, we cannot provide, modify, or delete data we do not hold. For data access, correction, or deletion requests related to your purchase, please contact Paddle directly at paddle.com/legal/privacy.
