Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 28, 2026

What We Do Not Collect

ExcelSQLMaster runs entirely on your machine. The software does not collect, transmit, or store any user data. There is no telemetry, no analytics, no usage tracking, and no cloud processing. Your data never leaves your machine.

We do not operate servers that store customer information. We do not maintain a user database.

License Verification

When you open ExcelSQLMaster, the software contacts Paddle to verify that your license key is active. No personal data is sent — only your license key is transmitted to Paddle for validation.

Payment and Customer Data

All purchases, billing, and customer data are handled entirely by Paddle.com Market Limited, who acts as the Merchant of Record. Paddle collects your name, email address, and payment information. We do not have access to your payment details. Paddle's privacy policy governs how they handle your data at paddle.com/legal/privacy.

Software Delivery

After purchase, Paddle sends you an email with a download link and your license key. We do not handle the delivery process.

Cookies

This website does not use cookies.

Your Rights

Since we do not store your personal information, data access or deletion requests should be directed to Paddle at paddle.com/legal/privacy. For general inquiries, contact us at contact@excelsqlmaster.com.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
